Refund Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

14-day money-back guarantee

If Beacon isn't for you, email support@beaconmac.com within 14 days of purchase and we'll refund you in full — no questions asked. Refunds are issued back to your original payment method via Stripe, our payment processor (typically within 5–10 business days).

Cancelling your subscription

Cancel anytime from the app's Manage Subscription button or the Stripe customer portal. Cancelling stops future charges; your subscription keeps working until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Renewals

Subscriptions renew every 6 months. Stripe emails a receipt for each renewal. If you were charged and didn't mean to renew, contact us within 14 days of the charge and we'll refund it.

Contact

support@beaconmac.com — we usually reply within one business day.
